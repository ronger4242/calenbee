Calenbee Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Calenbee is an iPhone app that turns photos, voice notes, and typed notes into calendar events. This policy describes what data Calenbee accesses and how it is used.

Google account and calendar data

When you sign in with Google, Calenbee receives your name, email address, and profile picture to identify your account. With your permission, Calenbee uses two Google Calendar permissions:

Read access is used to list your calendars so you can choose where events are saved, and to find existing events when you ask to move or cancel one.

Write access is used to create, update, or delete only the events you explicitly review and approve in the app.

Your calendar data is accessed directly between your device and Google. Calenbee's servers do not store your calendar events, and your Google data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Calendar event details you ask to match (titles, dates, calendar names) are sent to our server transiently to identify the event you meant, and are not retained.

Photos, voice recordings, and typed notes

When you scan a photo, record a voice note, or type a note, it is sent to Calenbee's server and processed with Google's Gemini AI to extract event details. These inputs are processed transiently and are not stored by Calenbee after processing.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store and are tied to your Apple Account. Calenbee verifies your subscription status using Apple's signed transaction data and does not receive your payment details.

Data protection and security

Calenbee protects your data using the following mechanisms:

Encryption in transit. All data sent between the Calenbee app, Calenbee's servers, and Google APIs — including photos, voice recordings, notes, and calendar requests — is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS). No user data is ever transmitted unencrypted.

Secure credential storage. Your Google sign-in tokens are stored only on your device and are never stored on Calenbee's servers. Calenbee never receives or stores your Google password, and OAuth tokens are never written to server databases or logs.

Transient processing. Photos, voice recordings, and text you submit are processed in memory to extract event details and are discarded when processing completes. They are not written to persistent storage, backed up, or reused.

Access controls. Access to Calenbee's server infrastructure is restricted to the developer and protected by authentication. Sensitive configuration values, such as API keys, are stored as protected environment secrets rather than in code.

Data minimization. Calenbee requests only the two Google Calendar permissions required for its features and accesses only the data needed to carry out the action you request.

No AI/ML training. Data obtained through Google APIs is never used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Data retention and deletion

Calenbee keeps minimal account data (your Google account identifier) to operate the service. You can revoke Calenbee's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately prevents any further calendar access. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Calenbee's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
