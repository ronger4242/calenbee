Why Calenbee exists

Built by a parent, for parents. 🐝

No more typing events into your calendar. Snap it — or just say it.

School flyers, birthday invitations, handwritten notes — Calenbee turns any photo into calendar events. Or just tell it: "Chess class every Sunday 6–7pm until Dec 14." Done.

I built Calenbee because, as a data scientist and a mom of two, I was tired of manually typing every school calendar and soccer schedule into my phone.

Every school year starts the same way: a stack of flyers, a soccer schedule, a class calendar taped to the fridge — and an evening lost to typing it all into a phone, one event at a time. Calenbee started as a small tool to get that evening back.

It does one thing well: it reads whatever life hands you — a photo, a screenshot, a spoken sentence — finds the events inside, and puts them where they belong. You review everything before it touches your calendar. Nothing is added without your OK.

— Shengnan Zhao, creator of Calenbee
